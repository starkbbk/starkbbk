## Formal verification

### [Multiset Descent](https://github.com/starkbbk/lean-multiset-descent)

Lean 4 and Mathlib formalization of Goodstein’s theorem and Kirby–Paris hydra termination using nested multiset orders. The project also proves exact costs and sharp worst-case bounds for branching worklists.

**135 theorems across 12 proof modules.** [Proof walkthrough](https://github.com/starkbbk/lean-multiset-descent/blob/main/docs/REVIEWER_GUIDE.md) · [GitHub Actions](https://github.com/starkbbk/lean-multiset-descent/actions/workflows/ci.yml)

### [Lean Quorum](https://github.com/starkbbk/lean-quorum)

Lean 4 and Mathlib proofs of Byzantine quorum intersection, weighted certificate agreement, and safety of an executable vote collector over arbitrary finite traces.

**61 theorems across 8 proof modules.** [Proof walkthrough](https://github.com/starkbbk/lean-quorum/blob/main/docs/REVIEWER_GUIDE.md) · [GitHub Actions](https://github.com/starkbbk/lean-quorum/actions/workflows/ci.yml)

Both projects disclose AI-assisted development and provide pinned dependencies, source-level theorem indexes, and reproducible proof audits.
